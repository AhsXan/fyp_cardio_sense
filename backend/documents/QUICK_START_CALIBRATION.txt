💡 WHY THIS WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Temperature Scaling Formula:
  P(y=i|x) = exp(z_i/T) / Σ_j exp(z_j/T)

Where:
- z_i are logits (pre-softmax outputs)
- T is temperature
- T = 1: standard softmax
- T > 1: "softens" probability distribution (less confident)

Label Smoothing:
  Hard labels [0, 1] → Soft labels [0.1, 0.9]
  Model can never reach 100% confidence during training

Class Weights:
  If NORMAL:ABNORMAL = 1:3 (imbalanced)
  Apply weights {0: 3.0, 1: 1.0} to balance
